<h3 id="h_44898827832661753161725968"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Content Filtering</span></h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Witchsky only pulls posts that contain specific hashtags, such as #WitchSky, #SpiritSky, #PaganSky, #OccultSky, #EsotericSky. It originally also contained #TarotSky and #AstrologySky. The use of hashtags, and with the “-sky” suffix, is intentional. This helps transform the feed into a community.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">You can also use /witchsky or /tarotsky if you want to avoid having your post appear in hashtag searches, or to help differentiate between Witchsky as a tag search and Witchsky as a feed.</span></p>
<h3 id="h_9658782813191753242314816"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Community vs Feed</span><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;"></span></h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">I could have easily created a “set it and forget it” feed, simply using the basic terms “witch”, “pagan”, etc. and even kept it to myself. However, I wanted to build a community space, not just a feed. I wanted buy-in, intentional conversation, and to build rapport with like-minded Bluesky users. So I created Witchsky, with all its complicated hashtags, and patiently and consistently promoted it until it bloomed into what it is today. And with any intentional creation, its purpose becomes distorted once the greater public becomes involved.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">By creating a feed that relies on very specific hashtags, rather than simply pulling the terms “pagan”, “witch”, “tarot”, etc, folks are intentionally adding their post to the feed. And that intention means purposeful interaction, and purposeful interaction generates a community space. And with any community space, that means community feedback and etiquette.</span></p>
<h3 id="h_8611535314861753242327984"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Community Growth</span></h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">The Witchsky feed was constructed and nurtured in an intentional way, to build community. As it has grown, it benefits from stewardship and feedback, so it can continue to be a safe and engaging space for the broader esoteric community.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">As Bluesky is small compared to other platforms, and the esoteric community is even smaller, the original Witchsky feed was (and broadly still is) a home for all esoteric-adjacent topics, including divination; religion, paganism and spirituality; astrology; occultism; and more. As one topic dominates the feed, it may be pushed to a secondary feed. This is so each sub-community can naturally grow and allow for easy access to the content and community folks are looking for. This growth and separation is what led to the creation of Tarotsky.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Tarotsky specifically was created due to an overwhelming number of Tarot of the Day posts, making it hard to find and engage in other forms of Witchsky content. A separate feed was created so folks could easily access both forms of content without competing for attention.</span></p>
<h3 id="h_73079616016241753243107319"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;"></span>Etiquette</h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Being that Witchsky and Tarotsky operate more like a community than a feed, there’s some etiquette involved to help keep the space safe, curated, accessible and functional. Note that moderation actions are limited so these are mostly voluntary recommendations, unfortunately.</span></p>
<ol>
<li value="1"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Please use alt-text for all images if you are able. There are some resources and tools available on Bluesky to that end, <a href="#h_3596662757531753242441284">please take time to research them</a>.</span></li>
<li value="2"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Cultural appropriation is not welcome in these spaces.</span></li>
<li value="3"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Harmful and fascist rhetoric, including racism, sexism and transphobia are not tolerated.</span></li>
<li value="4"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">AI generated content; recycled, uncredited, scraped, unoriginal graphics, art &amp; information; and spamming the feeds is not appreciated.</span></li>
</ol>
<h3 id="h_96260669118651753243116901"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">The Algorithm</span></h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">The Witchsky feeds utilize varying degrees of "HN rank" algorithms to filter and populate posts. The feeds still heavily prioritize new posts over popular posts, but the feeds are not strictly chronological and there is not a guarantee that your post will generate into the feed right away - or at all. The HN rank factors age and engagement.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">If you're struggling to get engagement on your posts, try e</span><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">ngaging with others' posts to build rapport and like-minded followers/mutuals.</span><span style="text-wrap-mode: wrap;"> </span><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Your posts will then generate engagement from your followers, which will increase your odds of appearing in the Witchsky feeds.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">The purpose of the HN ranking is to help prioritize quality over quantity.</span></p>
<h3 id="h_49327557420701753243125546"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Tips for Using</span></h3>
<ol>
<li value="1"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Tarot posts are discouraged in the Witchsky feed. Any post that uses #Tarotsky or #Tarot will not appear in Witchsky. Please use the Tarotsky feed for things like Tarot of the Day, showing off your decks, or other content related to tarot readings. Obviously, there is some educational-focused content that may overlap in relevance - to appear in both feeds, you would need to use either the Witchsky tag(s) or the Tarotsky tag, and then reply or quote your post with the other tag.</span></li>
<li value="2"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">The #AstrologySky has been removed from the RegEx (but not blocked like Tarotsky). This is to reduce the number of posts from folks with only a passive or secular interest in astrology that are just randomly using the tag from crowding the feed. Professional &amp; witchy astrologers are still welcome, but would benefit from using #Witchsky + your preferred astrology tags. I have no plans to make an AstrologySky feed - there are several existing astrology feeds already in circulation.</span></li>
<li value="3"><strong><span style="white-space-collapse: preserve;">I </span><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">highly </span></strong><span style="white-space-collapse: preserve;"><strong>recommend using the feed just as a way to find folks you might want to connect with</strong>. Look for people that seem to align with your own beliefs and practices, or demonstrate knowledge in an area you have interest in. You can follow these folks, or add them to your own List to create a stream of all their posts (whether they include the tags or not!)</span></li>
<li value="4"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Please do not tag the Witchsky <em>account</em> </span><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">directly in your post when trying to add to the feed. Only using the <a href="#h_44898827832661753161725968">hashtags</a> listed above will populate your post to the feed.</span></li>
</ol>
<h3 id="h_28137611522211753243133479"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Safety</span></h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Note that this is not a forum like Reddit or Discord where I can fully moderate content and users. Please exercise your own moderation tools or create your own custom version of the feed to suit your goals.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">The witchcraft and adjacent communities are known to be home to charlatans, cults, and other varieties of harmful people, beliefs, practices and groups. Please be careful with who and how you engage.</span></p>
<ul>
<li value="1"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Stay informed of what is harmful (such as cultural appropriation).</span></li>
<li value="2"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Recognize dogwhistles and warning signs that may indicate a person, group or belief should be avoided or approached carefully.</span></li>
<li value="3"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Exercise caution and discernment.</span></li>
<li value="4"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Take steps to protect yourself such as maintaining boundaries, keeping personal details private, and utilizing moderation tools.</span></li>
</ul>
<ol>
<li value="1"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">If users are violating </span><a href="https://bsky.social/about/support/community-guidelines" rel="noreferrer"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Community Guidelines</span></a><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">, report them to Bluesky.</span></li>
<li value="2"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Mute keywords and users! Check out the moderation settings to help you independently filter content you’re not interested in from the feed, and mute folks who routinely post things you’d rather not engage with.</span></li>

<h3 id="h_61118611223961753243140965"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">Starter Packs and Related Feeds</span></h3>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">There are several variations of the Witchsky and Tarotsky feeds available to suit a range of content preferences. To find them, visit the Witchsky account</span><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;"> and navigate to the 'feeds' tab to find the comprehensive list.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">You can find feeds by searching terms in the Bluesky navigation bar and toggling to the 'feeds' tab of the search results. Feeds can also be found, pinned, deleted and moved in the feed settings (the giant # button). You can also use third-party search options such as </span><a href="https://goodfeeds.co/" rel="noreferrer"><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">goodfeeds.co</span></a><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">There is not an "official" Witchsky starter pack.</span></p>
<p><span style="white-space: pre-wrap;">You can find starter packs by navigating to the 'starter packs' tab of your favorite Bluesky (or Witchsky) user profile. Not everyone will have one, but starter packs are intended as a list of recommended profiles to follow. If you like one user, you might like the folks on that user's starter pack.</span></p>
